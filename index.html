[Before you send] // Frames in Delhi guidelines
  1. Send only photos you took yourself.
  2. One to three photos per message is plenty.
  3. Wide street scenes are great. Please skip close-ups of strangers’ faces.
  4. Featured photos may be reposted on Frames in Delhi and Delhi Adda, always with your credit.
  5. Changed your mind? Message us and we’ll take it down.
No cancellations needed. Just message us.
