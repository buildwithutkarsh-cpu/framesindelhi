Before you send

  • Send only photos you took yourself.
  • One to three photos per message is plenty.
  • Wide street scenes are great. Please skip close-ups of strangers’ faces.
  • Featured photos may be reposted on Frames in Delhi and Delhi Adda, always with your credit.
  • Changed your mind? Message us and we’ll take it down.
